Privacy Policy for Wedding Planner

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Wedding Planner ("the app") is developed by Focustech ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Information We Collect

Account Information

The app uses anonymous sign-in for authentication. We do not require or collect your email address, phone number, or password to create an account. When you first open the app, an anonymous account is created for you, and you choose a display name that is shown to any collaborators you invite to your wedding.

Wedding Planning Data

To provide the app's features, we store the information you and your collaborators enter, including:

  • Guest list details (names, phone numbers, addresses, RSVP status, meal preferences, notes)
  • Budget items and amounts
  • Vendor names, contact details, and notes
  • Checklist tasks, timeline/schedule events, and seating arrangements
  • Menu items, custom lists, and emergency contacts you add
  • Mood board photos you choose to add from your device's photo library

This data is stored to provide the app's core functionality (planning your wedding and, if you choose, collaborating with others on it) and is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Device Permissions

The app requests the following permissions, used only for their stated purpose:

  • Photos/Media - to let you attach photos to your mood board.
  • Notifications - to remind you about checklist tasks and schedule events you set.

How We Store Your Data

Wedding planning data you enter is stored using Supabase, a third-party backend service, so that it can sync across your devices and be shared with collaborators you explicitly invite. Some data (such as mood board photos and local app preferences) may also be stored locally on your device.

Data Sharing

Your wedding data is only visible to you and to collaborators you explicitly invite via an invite code. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. We may share data with service providers (such as our hosting and backend provider) solely to operate the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual items, delete an entire wedding, or reset all data from within the app at any time (Settings > Delete & Reset). Deleting a wedding permanently removes its associated data from our servers. You may also request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below, or see our account deletion instructions for full details.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

Delete Your Account & Data - Wedding Planner

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Wedding Planner (developed by Focustech) does not use email/password accounts — each install is signed in anonymously, and you choose a display name. You can delete your wedding data at any time, either from within the app or by contacting us directly.

Deleting some of your data (no account deletion required)

You don't need to delete your whole account or wedding to remove specific information. From within the app you can permanently delete, at any time:

  • An individual guest, including their name, phone number, address, and RSVP status
  • An individual budget item, vendor, checklist task, timeline event, menu item, or emergency contact
  • An individual mood board photo
  • An individual custom list or a single item within one

Just open the item and choose Delete (usually via a trash icon or a swipe/long-press action). This removes that item immediately and does not require deleting your account or the wedding it belongs to.

Deleting an entire wedding or all of your data

  1. Open Wedding Planner.
  2. Open the menu and go to Settings > Delete & Reset.
  3. To delete one wedding: tap Delete Wedding. This permanently removes that wedding and all of its checklist, budget, guest, vendor, timeline, seating, menu, mood board, and other associated data.
  4. To delete everything on your device/account: tap Reset All Data. This permanently removes every wedding you own and all associated data.

Both actions require confirmation and cannot be undone.

Option: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email pipansrkr@gmail.com and describe what you'd like deleted — specific data (e.g. a guest's contact details, a photo) or everything associated with your account. Include the display name and, if known, the wedding/invite details so we can locate your data. We will process the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous account, display name, and profile record.
  • All wedding data you own: guest list entries (names, phone numbers, addresses, RSVP status), budget items, vendor details, checklist tasks, timeline/schedule events, seating arrangements, menu items, custom lists, emergency contacts, and mood board photos.
  • Your membership and access records for any shared weddings you collaborated on (your own edits' attribution may be anonymized rather than deleted if removing them would break another owner's wedding data).

Retention

Deleted data is removed from our active database immediately. Routine backups may retain deleted data for up to 30 days before being purged.
